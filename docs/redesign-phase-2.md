# Phase 2 — Redesign foundation

## Positioning

Stanford Development Solutions is an independent web and growth partner for local service businesses. The site should sell a practical outcome—more qualified calls and quote requests—rather than a list of technical deliverables.

The public brand is operated by Kade Stanford as an independent freelancer. Copy must not imply that Stanford Development Solutions is an LLC, corporation, registered agency, or separate legal entity unless that changes later.

## Primary audience

An owner or operator of a local service business whose website, search presence, or advertising is not producing enough measurable leads. They value clarity, responsiveness, and a partner who can handle the work without an agency-sized process.

## Primary journey

1. Understand the outcome in the first screen.
2. See credible, real work.
3. Understand the services and working process.
4. Gain confidence in Kade as the direct point of contact.
5. Request a free local audit.

The free audit is the primary conversion. Email and client login remain secondary paths.

## Information architecture

- Home
  - Outcome-led introduction
  - Selected work and tangible proof
  - Services organized around the customer journey
  - Simple engagement process
  - Founder credibility
  - Free-audit form
- Contact / free audit
- Client login and dashboard
- Privacy policy
- Terms of service
- Case studies when real content is ready

## Messaging principles

- Lead with business outcomes, then explain the work.
- Use real projects and verifiable facts; do not invent performance numbers.
- Replace broad claims with concrete next steps.
- Keep the founder-led nature of the business visible.
- Use “free local audit” consistently for the main call to action.

## Visual direction

Confident and technical without feeling like a software template: deep navy surfaces, crisp blue accents, editorial typography, restrained grid geometry, real project screens, and minimal motion. The interface should feel direct, capable, and local-business friendly.

## Performance guardrails

- No WebGL or continuously animated canvas in the critical first viewport.
- One priority image in the hero.
- Keep essential copy and primary actions server-rendered.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences.
- Target mobile LCP below 2.5 seconds and minimal main-thread blocking.

## Phase 2 implementation sequence

1. Replace the homepage hero and clarify the primary conversion.
2. Simplify navigation and remove competing actions. Completed initial navigation pass with Work, Services, Pricing, About, and one Free Audit CTA.
3. Reframe portfolio content as evidence-based case studies. Completed initial Big Bass and Liberty House case studies; add verified results and client quotes when available.
4. Reorganize services around diagnose, build, launch, and measure.
5. Shorten and simplify the audit form journey. Completed initial single-form pass; detailed discovery now happens after contact.
6. Apply the visual system consistently to supporting public pages.

Founder credibility now uses a direct, evidence-based About section: Kade is identified as the hands-on point of contact, his Bachelor of Science in Information Technology from Southeastern Louisiana University (December 2025) is stated accurately, and the client process is explained in four steps.

## Offer and pricing direction

- One-page Launch Page: from $1,000.
- Local Business Website: from $2,500.
- Lead-Generation Website: from $3,500.
- Website Care & Hosting: $175/month with two small requests or 60 minutes of edits included.
- Google Ads: $1,250 setup and $600/month, excluding ad spend.
- Google + Meta: $1,750 setup and $900/month, excluding ad spend.
- Broader content, review, reactivation, and rapid-response work: Growth Partner scope from $1,250/month.

These are public starting points, not fixed quotes. Every engagement still receives a written scope. Revisit the numbers after several projects using estimated hours, actual delivery time, close rate, and client value—not competitor pricing alone.
