# Service scope and pricing baseline

This is the working commercial baseline for new proposals. It is not a signed client agreement and should be reviewed before being turned into contract language.

## Website projects

### Local Starter - $600 fixed project price

> A straightforward one-page website using a reusable layout customized to your business branding.

- One page with up to five sections
- Client-approved business information, photos, and text, with light organization
- Mobile-friendly presentation, click-to-call, and a simple inquiry form
- Basic page title, description, search setup, and launch
- One-time Google Business Profile setup assistance for eligible businesses; owner verification required
- One-time domain-based business email setup assistance
- One consolidated revision round
- $300 deposit applied to the total; $300 after preview approval and before launch

> You keep ownership and admin control of your Business Profile, domain, and email accounts. Google controls profile verification; search rankings are not guaranteed. Email-provider subscription or license fees and domain registration and renewal are paid separately.

> Additional pages, stores, booking systems, portals, integrations, and ongoing SEO are quoted separately. The build is paired with $75 per month hosting and maintenance after launch.

### Launch Page - from $1,000

- One conversion-focused page.
- Mobile and desktop design.
- Contact form, analytics, basic metadata, and launch.
- Client supplies final business facts, photographs, policies, and approvals.

### Local Business Website - from $2,500

- Up to five core pages.
- Custom design and development.
- Contact form, analytics, lead tracking, and on-page SEO foundation.
- Reasonable copy organization and editing from client-provided information.
- Additional service-area pages, long-form copywriting, portals, ecommerce, booking systems, and custom integrations are separate.

### Lead-Generation Website - from $3,500

- Expanded service/location structure and campaign landing pages.
- Conversion tracking and local-search foundations.
- Final scope depends on page count, content readiness, integrations, and campaign needs.

### Website Hosting & Maintenance — $75 per month

> The standard ongoing service for websites I build and manage, starting after launch. Includes managed hosting, deployment, basic availability monitoring, routine maintenance, and small updates to existing text, photos, business hours, or contact details.

> New whole pages, added content sections, major redesigns, integrations, and expanded features need a separate scope and quote. Site requirements and unusual third-party costs are agreed in the written proposal.

> Your domain and connected accounts stay client-owned. Domain registration and renewal and email-provider subscription or license fees are paid separately. Ongoing SEO campaigns and emergency or same-day support are not included.

This is the standard ongoing plan for new SDS-built and managed sites. It does not change existing client contracts or billing.

Use this scope for the broader work described in the Big Bass proposal. Define exact monthly quantities for social posts, graphics, review replies, customer outreach, meetings, and rapid-response availability. Price extraordinary availability and multi-channel content explicitly.

### Creative production

- Static advertising graphics: from $250 per defined batch.
- Video editing, reels, photography, and ongoing social content: quoted by quantity and complexity.
- State the included number of concepts and revision rounds in every proposal.

## Proposal protections

Every proposal should state:

- Exact deliverables and exclusions.
- Client responsibilities and approval deadlines.
- Number of revision rounds.
- Payment schedule and late-payment rules.
- Who owns each account and pays third-party charges.
- No guarantee of rankings, lead volume, sales, or revenue.
- Advertising projections are estimates based on stated assumptions.
- Change requests outside scope require written approval and additional payment.
- Cancellation, handoff, and access-removal procedures.

## Pricing review

Record estimated hours, actual hours, revision count, support usage, and client outcome for every engagement. Review prices after the next three completed projects or six months, whichever comes first.
