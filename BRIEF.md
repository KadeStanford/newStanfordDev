# Stanford Development Solutions — Business Brief

Status: Approved by Kade Stanford on September 10, 2026.

## Positioning

Kade Stanford is an independent web developer based near Hammond, Louisiana, serving small businesses locally and remotely.

The site should feel personal and credible without presenting Stanford Development Solutions as a large agency or a formally registered company.

## Intended clients

The primary audiences are:

- Contractors and home-service businesses
- Lawn, landscaping, and garden businesses
- Small restaurants, cafes, and food businesses
- Other small businesses that need a useful website or a simpler way to handle part of their work

The site should lead with Hammond, Tangipahoa Parish, and Southeast Louisiana for local relevance while making it clear that remote projects are welcome. It should not try to position the business as serving everyone everywhere.

## Services

### Primary services

- Website design and development
- Website hosting, maintenance, and requested updates
- Google Ads management
- Meta Ads management

### Supporting services

- Graphics and advertising creative
- Custom business tools when a normal marketing website is insufficient

Custom tools should be described through plain-language examples, including:

- Online estimates or quote requests
- Contracts and electronic signatures
- Invoice creation
- Customer or employee portals
- Lead organization
- Replacing repetitive paperwork or manual processes

Visitors should be invited to describe what slows them down rather than being expected to know technical terminology or specify a technical solution.

## Main visitor action

The recommended primary action is to request a free website review or project conversation.

Kade will respond within 24 hours on weekdays. The response may be an email, phone call, short video, or another appropriate format. The site must not promise that every inquiry receives a formal report or video.

## Credibility boundaries

The site must not:

- Present Kade as a large agency or established team
- Imply that Stanford Development Solutions is formally registered
- Invent testimonials or client statements
- Claim advertising results before campaigns have produced documented results
- Invent traffic, lead, revenue, ranking, or conversion metrics
- Guarantee sales, rankings, or advertising outcomes
- Treat fast AI-assisted delivery as a substitute for explaining the value of the finished work
